KEY IDEA

The places where species are cared for and the habitats where they must ultimately survive are often hundreds of kilometres apart. Conservation is a coordinated network—not a single site.

BEHIND THE DATA

Programs do not all follow the same pathway. For example, Adelaide Zoo supports a Western swamp tortoise insurance population, while Australia Zoo's crocodile work centres on research and public education.

CASE STUDY / PERTH ZOO

A species brought back from the brink

The Western swamp tortoise was once believed extinct. Perth Zoo's breed-for-release collaboration has returned over 1,148 tortoises to protected habitat, according to its May 2026 update.

Small frogs, large recovery effort

How to read this project

Coverage. Six selected sites across five states, and thirteen examples of projects. Locations are approximate site coordinates, not conservation field-work sites.

Method. Each activity cell was manually coded from a cited source. Blank cells mean “not recorded in this small review,” not “the zoo does not perform this activity.” The release dot plot combines different published report dates; counts are not directly comparable.

Technical stack. Vega-Lite, Vega, Vega-Embed, HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Data is stored in local CSV and GeoJSON files for reproducibility.

Data accessed. October 2026. The Australian outline was created from the GSHHS low-resolution coastline distributed with Basemap; geographic boundaries are schematic.
